## Store listing tab

**Name** (from the manifest): Book Ratings for Audible

**Summary** (the manifest's description, at most 132 characters):

> Shows Goodreads ratings, genres and first-published years on Audible book
> cards. Not affiliated with Audible, Amazon or Goodreads.

**Description:**

```text
See how readers rate a book before you spend a credit on it.

Book Ratings for Audible adds each book's Goodreads rating to the Audible store: on search results, categories, deals, the home page, your Library and your Wish List.

Every book card gets:
• The Goodreads rating and number of ratings, such as ★ 4.21 (12k), in green for highly rated books and red for poorly rated ones. It links to the book on Goodreads.
• The year the book was first published, and up to three Goodreads genres.
• Goodreads' overview of the book when you hover over the rating.

It works on all ten Audible marketplaces: .com, .co.uk, .ca, .com.au, .in, .de, .fr, .it, .es and .co.jp.

Considerate by design
• Books are looked up only as they scroll into view, a few at a time, and results are cached on your device, so Goodreads isn't asked about the same book again for a month.
• If a book can't be matched with confidence, the badge says so and links to a Goodreads search instead of guessing.
• Turn it off and on, and clear the cache, from the toolbar button.

Private
• No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking.
• To look a book up, the extension sends its title and author to Goodreads, and the book's Audible ID to Audible's own catalogue. Nothing else leaves your browser, and the requests carry no cookies.

Free and open source (MIT): https://github.com/liqweed/audible-ex

Not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Audible, Amazon or Goodreads. Audible and Goodreads are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.
```

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose:**

> Shows each Audible book's Goodreads rating, number of ratings, genres and
> first-published year on the Audible pages where the book appears, linking to
> the book on Goodreads.

**Permission justifications:**

| Permission                                           | Justification                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `storage`                                            | Keeps the user's on/off setting in sync storage.                                                                                                                                                   |
| `unlimitedStorage`                                   | Keeps the local cache of Goodreads lookups (thousands of books for heavy users) so each book is looked up at most once a month instead of on every page view, sparing Goodreads repeated requests. |
| Host `https://www.goodreads.com/*`                   | The background worker looks books up with Goodreads' search (title and author) and reads the matched book's public page for its rating, genres and first-published year.                           |
| Hosts `https://api.audible.<tld>/*` (10)             | The background worker asks Audible's catalogue on the marketplace being browsed for the title and author of the books on the page, by their ASINs, to search Goodreads with.                       |
| Content script on `https://www.audible.<tld>/*` (10) | Finds the book cards on Audible pages and shows the rating badge on each.                                                                                                                          |

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. Everything is bundled in the
package.

**Data usage**, collected or transmitted:

- [x] **Website content**: the titles, authors and ASINs of the books on

- [ ] Every other category (personally identifiable information, health,

Certify all three: not sold or transferred to third parties outside the approved

## Assets

`bun e2e` builds the extension, loads `.output/chrome-mv3` into Playwright's
bundled Chromium, checks badges on live `audible.com` and `audible.co.uk`, and
writes the three screenshots and the promo tile to `.output/store/`. It opens a
browser window: Goodreads answers 403 to headless Chromium. Outside the US,
`audible.com` serves its error page for search and book pages, so the `.com`
check uses the home page and the screenshots come from `.co.uk`. Look at every
image before uploading: the pages show whatever Audible lists that day.

## Submitting a release

1. On an up-to-date `main` with the release commit tagged (`changes/README.md`),
   run `bun run build`, `bun e2e`, then `bun crx`, which signs the build with
   the key from 1Password; the upload is
   `.output/audible-ex-<version>-chrome.crx`. (Before the opt-in, `bun zip`'s
   `.output/audible-ex-<version>-chrome.zip` is accepted too.)
2. First release only: register at
   https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole with a Google account that has
   2-step verification, pay the one-time US$5 fee, verify the contact email,
   and declare non-trader status (a free, non-commercial extension).
3. Dashboard → **Items** → **New item** (later releases: the item → **Package**
   → **Upload new package**), upload the package.
4. Fill the three tabs from this file; upload the assets.
5. **Submit for review.** After approval, install from the store link and check
   badges on two marketplaces.

## Automated releases

`.github/workflows/release.yml` does steps 1 and 3–5 of "Submitting a release"
for every release after the first (DECISIONS.md D20). A person runs the release
commit (`changes/README.md`) and pushes `vx.y.z`; nothing else is asked of
them (DECISIONS.md D21). The run checks that the tag
matches `package.json` and is on `main`, gates, builds, signs (`sign` job),
uploads and submits (`publish` job) and attaches the zips to a GitHub release.
The store then reviews it as usual; read the result in the dashboard.

One-time setup, in this order (not yet done):

1. **Opt in to Verified CRX uploads** (D18, after 0.1.0's first review). Put the
   private key from 1Password into the repository's secret `CWS_SIGNING_KEY`,
   under the `release-signing` environment, not the repository's secrets.
2. **Environments** (Settings → Environments): `release-signing` and
   `chrome-web-store`, each with *Deployment branches and tags* limited to the
   tag pattern `v*`, and no required reviewers.
3. **A ruleset on `v*` tags** (Settings → Rules) that restricts who can create
   them to the owner.
4. **Google Cloud**: a project with the Chrome Web Store API enabled; a service
   account; a Workload Identity pool and provider for GitHub with the attribute
   condition `assertion.repository == 'liqweed/audible-ex'` and
   `assertion.environment == 'chrome-web-store'` and
   `assertion.job_workflow_ref` starting with
   `liqweed/audible-ex/.github/workflows/release.yml@refs/tags/v`; the role
   *Workload Identity User* on the service account for that provider's principal.
5. **Add the service account to the store publisher** (developer dashboard →
   Account → the publisher's service accounts), and note the publisher ID
   (Account page).
6. **Repository variables** (not secrets): `GCP_WORKLOAD_IDENTITY_PROVIDER`
   (`projects/…/locations/global/workloadIdentityPools/…/providers/…`),
   `GCP_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` (its email) and `CWS_PUBLISHER_ID`.
7. **Try the credentials without releasing.** `gcloud auth print-access-token
   --impersonate-service-account` drops the scope, so mint the token with the
   scope the workflow asks for (needs *Service Account Token Creator* on the
   service account for your own account):

   ```sh
   SA=cws-publisher@audible-ex.iam.gserviceaccount.com
   TOKEN=$(curl -s -X POST "https://iamcredentials.googleapis.com/v1/projects/-/serviceAccounts/${SA}:generateAccessToken" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $(gcloud auth print-access-token)" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d '{"scope":["https://www.googleapis.com/auth/chromewebstore"]}' | jq -r .accessToken)
   bun run build && bun crx
   CWS_ACCESS_TOKEN=$TOKEN CWS_PUBLISHER_ID=<id> bun scripts/upload-cws.ts --dry-run .output/audible-ex-<version>-chrome.crx
   ```

   It checks the signature, the token and the item's state without uploading.
   Done on 2026-11-03: the service account is accepted by the store (it was
   added in the dashboard's Account tab) and the item reports `PUBLISHED`.

Not verified against the live store: the upload itself (`X-Goog-Upload-Protocol:
raw` with a CRX under API v2), the Workload Identity exchange from GitHub, and
that Chrome's `--pack-extension` runs on the CI runner (it does on a Mac). Watch
the first automated release and fix what it shows; the manual route above still
works. A submission already in review makes the run stop with a message (cancel
it in the dashboard, then re-run the job).
