### Changed

- Internal: the extension can now look up a book's title and author in
  Audible's catalogue and read its Goodreads rating, genres, first-published
  year and overview, with no cookies sent to either site. Audible book IDs
  (ASINs) go to the catalogue of the marketplace you are browsing, and book
  titles and authors to Goodreads, as the privacy statement says. The
  permissions are the ones already requested. Nothing changes on Audible pages
  yet.
