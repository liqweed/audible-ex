### Added

- The panel can be moved: drag its title to any corner of the page and it
  docks there, remembered on this device. A `–` button collapses it to a small
  pill showing the count (`34/60`); click the pill to open it again. A `×`
  button hides the panel on that Audible site, and the toolbar popup shows
  "Show the panel again" until you bring it back.
