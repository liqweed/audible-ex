### Added

- After a few days of use, once the lookups on a page are done, the panel (or the toolbar popup) asks once whether you would rate the extension in the Chrome Web Store (Firefox Add-ons in Firefox). It waits until you have seen ratings on three different days and either changed a filter, highlight or display setting or seen 200 ratings. Either button ends it for good, and it offers nothing in return.
- "Rate it" links in the toolbar popup and on the settings page, for anyone who wants to rate without waiting to be asked.

### Changed

- The extension now counts, on your device only, the days it showed ratings and how many ratings it showed, to decide when to ask. Nothing is sent anywhere; the counts are deleted with the extension. The privacy statement says so.
- On Firefox, the settings page now links to the Firefox Add-ons page.
